Who each package is for

Basic

Applicants who need a credible first view on whether their profile is strong enough and what would improve the case.

Standard

Applicants who already have substance and now need a structured evidence plan, clearer positioning, and stronger dossier logic.

Premium

Applicants who want one joined-up workflow for endorsement preparation and visa filing support with fewer handoff points.

Upgrade path

Basic → Standard: move from route clarity into a full evidence strategy when the profile is viable but under-structured.

Standard → Premium: add end-to-end coordination if you want continuity through endorsement assembly and visa filing.

Buying guidance

How to choose well.

  • Choose Basic if your main question is whether you have a credible route and what evidence would matter most.
  • Choose Standard if you already know the route is likely viable but need sharper criteria mapping and dossier structure.
  • Choose Premium if you want joined-up support across review, strategy, endorsement preparation, and visa-stage coordination.

FAQ

Are fees inclusive of government charges?

No. Package fees cover advisory and support services only. Government fees and any third-party costs are separate.

Can I start with Basic and upgrade later?

Yes. That route is useful when you want an expert first view before deciding whether a fuller strategy build is justified.

Does Premium guarantee success?

No. Premium gives the highest level of support and coordination, but endorsement and visa decisions remain outside our control.
