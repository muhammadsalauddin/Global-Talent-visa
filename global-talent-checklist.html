Printable evidence checklist

A client-ready checklist for building a stronger Global Talent file.

Use this checklist before the strategy review so the strongest career signals, documents, and recommenders can be identified early.

How to use

Print the page, mark what already exists, and note which items need stronger evidence, cleaner formatting, or clearer explanation.

1

CV and profile summary

Core profile documents

Current CV with dates, roles, promotions, and measurable achievements.

Short professional summary explaining your field, specialism, and seniority.

LinkedIn profile, portfolio, professional website, or public researcher page.

Concise list of the top five achievements you believe are most persuasive.

2

Recognition evidence

Awards, profile signals, and external validation

Awards, prizes, fellowships, grants, or formal recognition documents.

Media coverage, press mentions, interviews, or featured profiles.

Judging invitations, panel roles, conference speaking invitations, or keynote materials.

Independent references or notable public endorsements relevant to your field.

3

Career strength evidence

Seniority, impact, and commercial weight

Employment letters, offer letters, promotion evidence, or senior role confirmations.

Salary evidence where it meaningfully supports seniority or market position.

Project evidence showing scale, ownership, commercial impact, or innovation.

Metrics that demonstrate growth, revenue influence, user impact, adoption, or performance gains.

4

Research and output evidence

Publications, patents, and original work

Publications, citations, preprints, journal articles, or book chapters.

Patents, product launches, open-source work, or technical contributions with public proof.

Research summaries that explain why the work matters beyond a raw publication list.

Evidence of leadership in research programmes, labs, collaborations, or specialist initiatives.

5

Recommendation letters

Recommenders and letter planning

List of potential recommenders with role, organisation, and relationship to you.

Short note on what each recommender can credibly speak to.

Draft talking points or achievement highlights that each letter should reinforce.

Confirmation that recommenders can provide strong, personalised letters rather than generic character references.

6

Identity and narrative inputs

Personal statement and filing readiness

Valid passport scan and summary of immigration history where relevant.

Notes on why the UK is strategically relevant to your next stage of work.

Short explanation of current goals, target timeline, and any urgent constraints.

Questions or concerns you want clarified during the review.

Preparation notes

  • Prioritise evidence with independent validation over self-descriptions wherever possible.
  • Do not overload the file with repetitive exhibits; quality matters more than volume.
  • If a strong achievement is difficult to understand quickly, add a short summary explaining context and significance.

Next step

Bring this completed checklist into the consultation so the review can focus on signal strength, evidence gaps, and the right support tier.
