Printable application roadmap

The Global Talent journey from first review to visa filing.

This roadmap explains the work sequence, the purpose of each stage, and what success should look like before moving to the next step.

Best use

Use this as a client briefing tool so the process feels disciplined, staged, and evidence-led rather than mysterious.

01

Consultation

Establish route goals and case context.

Understand your field, current position, timing, and what you believe the strongest profile signals are.

Success looks like

A clear understanding of your objective, likely fit, and whether a deeper review is commercially sensible.

02

Route fit review

Check how the profile aligns with the route and endorsement lens.

Review the field, body of work, and available evidence to see where a credible Global Talent case may exist.

Success looks like

A confident go, no-go, or not-yet decision with rationale.

03

Profile strength assessment

Identify the signals with real persuasive power.

Separate high-value achievements from supporting background material so the strongest case pillars are obvious.

Success looks like

A shortlist of meaningful evidence themes that can carry the case.

04

Evidence mapping

Match documents to the role they need to play.

Assign each exhibit to a case function so evidence is purposeful, non-repetitive, and easy to interpret.

Success looks like

A structured evidence map with clear priorities and gaps.

05

Gap analysis

Strengthen weak areas before submission.

Flag missing proof, unsupported claims, and documents that need clearer explanation or stronger sourcing.

Success looks like

A realistic action list that improves case quality before assembly begins.

06

Statement and narrative positioning

Create a coherent case story.

Shape the narrative so achievements, trajectory, and future UK contribution connect clearly.

Success looks like

A statement structure that supports the evidence rather than repeating it.

07

Recommendation letter planning

Direct each recommender toward the right proof points.

Ensure letters are specific, credible, and aligned with the strongest parts of the case.

Success looks like

Letters that reinforce the submission logic instead of sounding generic.

08

Dossier assembly

Build the submission in a disciplined sequence.

Combine evidence, summaries, and letters into a clean structure that is easy to review and defend.

Success looks like

A submission-ready file with internal consistency and minimal duplication.

09

Endorsement submission

Submit the case when the logic is ready, not just when the forms are complete.

Final review focuses on coherence, evidential coverage, and avoiding avoidable weaknesses.

Success looks like

A credible endorsement file with strong signal density and clear positioning.

10

Visa filing

Carry the same discipline into the second stage.

Coordinate the visa-stage file so the narrative, documents, and timing remain consistent.

Success looks like

A clean second-stage submission that carries forward the quality of the endorsement work.

Next step

Use the roadmap to set expectations and keep the process evidence-led.
