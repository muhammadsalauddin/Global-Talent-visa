Best-fit client profile

  • Applicants with a credible body of work who need a better way to present and prioritise evidence.
  • Professionals whose achievements are real, but currently scattered across different documents and platforms.
  • Candidates who need stronger recommendation planning and a more defensible dossier structure.

What it prepares you for

A cleaner, stronger endorsement file with clearer evidence logic, better internal coherence, and fewer weak exhibits undermining the case.

Scope of work

  • Review the strongest career signals and match them to the role they need to play in the case.
  • Build an evidence map that prioritises stronger documents and deprioritises noise.
  • Run a gap analysis so missing proof, weak explanations, and unsupported claims are identified early.
  • Guide narrative positioning for the personal statement and the broader case story.
  • Plan how recommendation letters should support the strongest parts of the file.

Deliverables

  • Structured evidence strategy and prioritisation plan.
  • Gap analysis with practical actions to improve the case.
  • Statement positioning guidance and recommendation letter direction.
  • Dossier architecture advice so submission assembly is cleaner and easier to review.

Why clients choose Standard

  • They already have substance and do not need a basic eligibility-only conversation.
  • They want a case that looks structured and intentional rather than improvised.
  • They need to reduce uncertainty before moving to endorsement submission.
